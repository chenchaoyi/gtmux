## Why
User-provided HQ screenshots show entry at old conversation content, unclear composer destination wording, a disconnected and sparse activity list, and mismatched archive control typography. The user asks for a cohesive, user-facing review rather than isolated cosmetic fixes.

## What Changes
- Make opening/reselecting a conversation reveal the newest loaded content reliably while preserving deliberate historical reading during refresh/earlier-page loading.
- Replace internal routing prose with plain composer destination labels, preserving all existing scopes and drafts.
- Present workspace actions and journal events on one compact chronological timeline with local date/time, explicit related context and readable outcomes. Preserve each source record, stable identity, filters and bounded-history facts; do not invent summaries or regroup unrelated work.
- Align existing HQ action-control fonts/sizes and improve confirmed reading/disclosure inconsistencies using shared components.
- Add isolated projection/navigation/layout regressions and hosted synthetic visual evidence. Never manipulate the real HQ or personal records.

## Surfaces
- terminal: protocol/data unchanged; no new command or session action.
- menubar: this change targets the retained native HQ workspace and its resource views.
- phone: no new workspace surface; existing receipt/access-history corrections remain in the prior PR.
- iPad: same mobile boundary as phone; native chronology is designed for future reuse of concepts, not a claim of iPad implementation.
- Web: no native workspace equivalent; existing HQ behavior unchanged.
