# Mac check details and screenshot editing

## Why
Expanded environment rows repeat their current result and sometimes their own
label/purpose. Screenshot notes are single-line. Screenshot images are fixed at
an initial 85% screen fit and do not follow window resizing.

## Changes
Show each check result once, retain distinct technical guidance, and expose long
results without a duplicate preview. Support multiline screenshot notes (Return
newline, Command-Return explicit send). Start at native point size when possible;
bound the window to its display and fit image/marks together to its actual canvas
when resized. Full-resolution exports and all send interlocks remain intact.

## Surfaces
- Terminal: doctor probes, classification and text report are unchanged.
- Menu bar: fix check detail repetition, screenshot note and responsive editor.
- Phone: not applicable; no local Mac screenshot window.
- iPad: not applicable; no local Mac screenshot window.
- Web: not applicable; no local Mac screenshot window.
