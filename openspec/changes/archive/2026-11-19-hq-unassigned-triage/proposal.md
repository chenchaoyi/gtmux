## Why

A worker can receive an instruction directly, stop with an open step, and have both events recovered by HQ's unfiltered delta. Without unique workspace attribution the checkpoint can advance while no durable HQ work remains.

## What Changes

- Persist a triage observation with its exact event citation and source identity when a managed terminal or explicitly followed native conversation supplies direct instruction evidence or a completion without a unique execution matter.
- Reuse the workspace cursor transaction, exact claims and wake retry. Add explicit HQ dispositions for verified no follow-up, a sourced future check or handoff to the current goal.
- Keep registration, responsibility, authority and delivery separate. A triage observation cannot create a goal, establish a sender, authorize submission or change user safeguards.

## Surfaces

- Terminal and remote attach use the shared CLI reconciliation queue and its HQ-only dispositions.
- macOS menubar keeps its existing wake presentation. HQ performs assessment in the terminal.
- Phone keeps its existing presentation and follow permission. HQ performs assessment in the terminal.
- iPad uses the same permissions and behavior as phone.
- Web keeps its existing matter presentation and access. HQ performs assessment in the terminal.
