# Clear settings hierarchy and interface terminology

## Why

Notification preferences flatten controls, permission state, test feedback and explanations into unrelated rows. Diagnostic and sharing labels use conversational phrases instead of identifiable settings and actions. Related surfaces drift in vocabulary.

## What changes

- Group notification controls and their status/test feedback; keep terminal-tab alerts separate.
- Use concise setting names, explicit permission descriptions and consistent action labels.
- Review built-in Mac, phone and web interface copy for similar issues. Preserve user content, historical release notes and compatibility/parser keys.
- Preserve notification timing, permissions, persistence, sharing scopes and backend protocols.

## Surfaces

- terminal: no command or output contract changes.
- menubar: notification/diagnostic preferences, sharing and related management copy.
- phone: equivalent management/status terminology.
- iPad: the shared phone implementation.
- web: equivalent sharing and management terminology where present.
