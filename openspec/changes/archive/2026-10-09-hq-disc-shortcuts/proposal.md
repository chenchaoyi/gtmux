## Why

The floating HQ entrance requires opening the HQ page before reaching Usage or Knowledge base. Owners need a direct long-press choice without confusing taps or repositioning the disc.

## What Changes

Add a two-action shortcut menu using the existing anchored menu with haptic recognition and accessible actions. Keep tap and drag gestures independent; cancel the hold timer on movement, interruption and unmount. Reuse existing Usage/Knowledge sheets on the HQ route, forwarding explicit requests through compact, regular and demo shells. Wait for the shortcut menu to dismiss before presenting a destination sheet on iOS.

## Surfaces

- terminal: unchanged; existing APIs are consumed.
- menubar: unchanged; existing HQ resource entrances remain.
- phone: long-press floating HQ for Usage or Knowledge base.
- iPad: compact windows share the floating control; regular sidebar retains its HQ card and resource doors.
- Web: unchanged.

## Impact

Mobile HQ gesture, workspace navigation, destination sheets and tests; bilingual docs/spec. No native dependency or server change. Shortcuts require an available HQ entrance; absent HQ retains its start explanation. Guests have no HQ disc.
