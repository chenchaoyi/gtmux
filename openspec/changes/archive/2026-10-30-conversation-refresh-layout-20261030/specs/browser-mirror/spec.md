## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: App-aligned session heading casing
The desktop and narrow browser rosters SHALL use the App's uppercase English category headings, including status and external categories. Chinese labels SHALL remain unchanged. Hide/Show actions SHALL keep sentence case, and inline session statuses SHALL retain the shared status vocabulary rather than inheriting heading capitalization.

#### Scenario: Category disclosure in either browser roster
- **WHEN** an English-speaking user opens or collapses a session section on desktop or mobile web
- **THEN** its category name matches the App's uppercase heading
- **AND** the disclosure action remains Hide or Show with its normal touch target
