Notifications
- Choose which Macs can send push notifications from the Servers list. Offline Macs show when a change is waiting to sync.

Codex conversations
- Codex replies appear in Chat during a task, without waiting for the turn to finish.
- Codex sessions outside tmux show their saved titles and identify whether they belong to a terminal or ChatGPT desktop. Desktop sessions no longer offer Move to tmux.
- Improved completion detection reduces incorrect status changes and extra notifications caused by usage warnings.

Connections and controls
- Routes show a loading state and display known routes sooner. Direct route selections are more reliable after switching.
- Control keys give tap feedback. Input history styling and long-press menus for sessions at the bottom of the list have been fixed.

Update gtmux on your Mac for the Codex status and Direct route improvements. The Mac companion now also supports the cmux terminal.
