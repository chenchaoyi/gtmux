Connection
- The phone reconnects after your Mac wakes or gtmux restarts. It can also find the Mac after its address changes.
- Settings shows the current route and its latency. You can switch routes from the phone; a failed switch keeps the previous route.

Chat and terminal
- Chats show whether a message came from you, HQ, or another agent. Older HQ replies keep the name of the agent that wrote them.
- Wide Codex screens keep their original Mac terminal width and can be scrolled horizontally.
- Send attachments up to 30 MB. HQ shows work that is still running or waiting for you.

Sharing and messages
- Share links display in full, with copy and system sharing options. Pairing and diagnostics messages explain what to do next.
