iPad support
- gtmux now runs on iPad as well as iPhone. On an iPad the session list sits on the left, and the session, HQ page or All panes you open shows on the right.
- With a hardware keyboard attached, hold ⌘ to see every shortcut.

HQ conversation
- Scroll up to see the conversation from before a /clear.
- Tasks HQ dispatched, sessions it reclaimed and the times it was woken appear in the conversation when they happened.
- Items HQ is waiting on you for have a Tell HQ button: accept its recommendation in one tap, or reply yourself.

Usage
- Plan bars change color with use: blue normally, amber once a weekly window passes 85%, red when a window is used up.
- Token use by day, shown as a calendar heatmap with today's and this week's totals and how many days in a row you have used it.
- Window names, reset times and machine warnings follow your phone's language.

Other improvements
- Interface text is plainer, and errors say what to do next.
- The HQ page's tiles and All panes show a loading indicator while they load.

Plus many bug fixes.
