Servers
- The Servers page shows which Mac you are on and whether each Mac answers. Hold a Mac and drag it to put your Macs in your own order.
- A Mac's ••• opens a menu to see its details, rename it on this phone, disconnect or remove it. Details shows the Mac's host name, system, chip, memory and uptime.
- Tap the Mac's name at the top of the radar to switch Mac.
- When a Mac has removed this phone, the app says access was rejected and asks you to pair again.

Sessions
- Start a new tmux session on your Mac from the radar or All panes. Its terminal opens right away.
- The terminal always fits the phone and redraws only the lines that change.
- The prompt Codex pins to the top of its screen shows in full in a bar above the terminal.
- When a session is asking you something or is still working, the app says so as you send.

Notifications and sharing
- Turning off a Mac's notifications also ends its Live Activity.
- A share link's code opens on the phone as a guest, and Sharing & pairing says when a change did not take, and why.

HQ
- The HQ page shows HQ's latest escalation, completion or brief. "What HQ did" lists its actions by day.

Other improvements
- The app works at the largest accessibility text sizes and in landscape.
- Plus many other fixes.

Details and new sessions need gtmux 1.0.95 or later on your Mac. Update it with gtmux update.
