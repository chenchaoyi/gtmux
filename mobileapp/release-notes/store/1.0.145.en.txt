Conversations and controls
• Answer permission requests and pickers directly in Chat. Use the key controls to continue when choices cannot be parsed.
• Send F1 through F12 and Ctrl, Alt or Shift combinations from the new Keys panel.
• Keep activity in a compact, fully collapsible list. Expand commands, delegated tasks and waits to inspect, scroll and copy their recorded details.
• Tap a participant avatar to inspect available agent versions, models, recorded plans and execution chains.

DeepSeek Harness and usage

Connections and interface

• Expired shares explain why access ended. External conversations filter transient calls and system content, and HQ messages keep their identity.
