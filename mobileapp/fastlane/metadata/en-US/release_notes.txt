Conversations
• Working sessions use a single progress line. Expanded Live previews stay open through refreshes.
• Pending questions appear above the conversation and remain accessible with the keyboard open.
• Unsent drafts stay with their server and session. Large-text prompts and collapse controls have improved layouts.

Knowledge and settings
• Knowledge entries show their intended use and progress, with clearer categories, sources and record details.
• Keep an entry open after changing its intended use or recording where it was applied.
• Settings, sharing, diagnostics and HQ use consistent labels. Send failures explain what happened and how to continue.

Update gtmux on your server to 1.0.149 or later to use all new features.
