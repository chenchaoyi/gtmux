# Maintenance and knowledge

## Consult before action

Before advice or dispatch, consult matching knowledge in the HQ home. Use `gtmux knowledge consult --query` with the current actor, project and known tool version. For example, use `--role hq --budget 1200` for the HQ's own decision. Read the full matching rule and exceptions, resolve `missing_required`, and carry only applicable facts with their IDs/revisions into worker goals. Search and inspect neighbours for gaps. Use legacy entries only as research. Query results and `self_report` usage do not prove a rule was followed or useful.

## Learn and land

Decide whether a correction, crash or recurrence yields reusable knowledge. Record the cue, actionable lesson, scope, exceptions and provenance, or explain why no durable fact is present. Capture on ordinary done/resolved only when useful. Search, inspect neighbours and improve an existing entry or supersede a conflict. Keep one current rule and link past evidence. Retire stale entries with a reason. Separate verified facts from hypotheses and retain evidence of competing cases. Use both languages where supported; a missing translation does not justify losing a verified lesson.

Use the knowledge CLI to update the ledger and verify the rendered view. Keep rendered topic files generated. Read `gtmux knowledge style` before writing. Use `gtmux knowledge` help for capture, add, amend or supersede, retire, promote, land and lint. Verify the rule reached the intended carrier and record the real reference. Match the audience: HQ standing policy in LOCAL, machine-wide cues in the managed global index, repo rules in their project, portable product behavior through project review. A promotion ticket is an open obligation until landed or withdrawn. Keep only reusable HQ knowledge tools in `knowledge/tools/`, with a matching entry describing when to run each. Lint identifies broken or orphaned tool references. Preserve private and excluded source boundaries at every step.

## Respond to maintenance triggers

On `[CONTROL gtmux:self-check]` or a matching wake, reconcile old pending decisions, board health, record hygiene and diagnostics. Read `gtmux knowledge lint` and inspect whichever finding needs action. On `[CONTROL gtmux:distill]` or its wake, reconcile the actual interval and pending captures using `gtmux capture`. A TRANSCRIPT MINER item is a lead. Use `gtmux knowledge mine --status` to inspect counts. Review each authorized lead with provenance, combine related keys, adopt with evidence or dismiss with reason. Preserve opt-outs and avoid scanning other native history just because a lead was supplied. Check overdue promotions and follow up with the actual owner. Gtmux owns scheduling. On a completed pass, return to the HQ root and use `gtmux hq --maintenance-done` for that trigger, including a completed no-change pass. Record substantive findings briefly and escalate critical ones.

For resumable review on a distill trigger, inspect `gtmux knowledge dream list` and status before opening a new bounded batch. Use `gtmux knowledge dream plan` to select; `reviewed_revisions` and the run history prevent repeat review. For `gtmux knowledge dream run`, record the chosen existing provider/model, reasoning, reason and timeout in project-owned storage. On interruption inspect `gtmux knowledge dream history`, verify the older attempt stopped, and resume only when the outcome is known or explicitly deferred. Preserve actual usage receipts. `usage_known:false` and `can_start:false` require resolving the relevant prerequisites. Respect call/time budgets and leave unknown token use unknown. Prefer checking the pending proposal over opening a new run. Run `gtmux knowledge review check` and authorized `gtmux knowledge review apply`; verify the resulting revision and leave an evidence reference. Use the repository's knowledge-review guide for examples and CLI help for the installed contract.
