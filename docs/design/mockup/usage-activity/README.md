# Usage · the year at a glance (usage-activity, 2026-09-15)

Five artboards from the design canvas the commander chose from, after 「现在只有一周，信息
不紧凑，而且感觉不够丰富」 with GitHub's contribution graph as the reference. `Main`
(direction A, daily heatmap), `ByWeek` and `Cumulative` are the three views that shipped
on the phone and the Mac reader; `Reader` is the Mac reader's 44-column version;
`DirectionB` (dense 30-day bars, no heatmap) is the alternative, kept with its reasons in
`canvas.json`'s notes. The greens are GitHub's five-step ramp at the commander's request.
Same format as `../gtmux-mobile.dc.html`; values lifted from `mobileapp/src/ui/theme.ts`
and `UsageSheet.tsx`. Re-seed with the `/design` helper to view; the seeded page is not
committed.
